# Competitor research — 21 October 2026

Official sources reviewed; descriptions establish capabilities, not comparative quality or a hands-on subscription test. No competitor paid account or YouTube video was required. AI features are outside scope.

| Capability | Official evidence / examples | Trader benefit | Journal2 baseline | Decision / priority / complexity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manual trade, notes, tags and detail review | [TraderSync tagging](https://tradersync.com/support/how-do-i-assign-a-setup-mistake-custom-tag-to-a-trade/), [Chartlog journal](https://www.chartlog.com/product/journal/) | Find and explain individual executions | Validated CRUD exists; four-step entry and disconnected search | Quick entry plus richer optional entry, shared filters/paging; P1 / medium |
| Review routines and next-period focus | [TradeZella weekly framework](https://www.tradezella.com/blog/weekly-trade-review-process), [TradesViz weekly view](https://www.tradesviz.com/blog/weekly-view/) | Turn observations into a repeatable review habit | No reviewed state or manual period review | Trade review queue and deterministic daily/weekly/monthly notebook; P1 / medium |
| Day notes versus reusable plans | [TradesViz notes/tags/plans](https://www.tradesviz.com/blog/when-to-use-trades-notes-tags-plans/), [Tradervue daily notes](https://www.tradervue.com/trading-journal-template) | Keep preparation, execution and reflection distinct | Daily boolean habits only | One notebook keyed by period, plan stored with settings; P1 / medium |
| Setup/mistake comparisons | [TraderSync dashboard](https://tradersync.com/support/how-can-i-see-my-best-setups-mistakes-and-tags-on-the-dashboard/), [Edgewonk features](https://edgewonk.com/features) | Identify outcome associations | Shared verified analytics already available | Preserve math, improve explanations and links to source rows; P1 / low |
| Calendar with day context | [Tradervue](https://www.tradervue.com/), [TraderSync calendar](https://tradersync.com/support/how-can-i-hide-the-setups-mistakes-from-the-calendar/) | Connect a day's outcome to trades and notes | Colored dots without selection or counts | Visible signed results/counts and day drilldown; P1 / medium |
| Goals / progress | [TradesViz goals](https://www.tradesviz.com/blog/trading-goals/), [Trademetria](https://trademetria.com/trading-journal) | Track a measurable process | No goal progress | Review-completion target, not profit promises; P2 / low |
| Fast review navigation and onboarding | [Edgewonk changelog](https://edgewonk.com/changelog) | Reduce friction and teach core workflow | Icon-only nav, no introduction | Labeled/collapsible nav, mobile menu, optional welcome checklist; P1 / medium |
| Search, global filters, account context | [Tradervue filtering](https://www.tradervue.com/trading-journal-template), [Trademetria features](https://trademetria.com/trading-journal) | Reach relevant evidence quickly | Analytics filters strong, journal list weak | Search/outcome/strategy/review filters and bounded list rendering; P1 / medium |
| Imports, broker connections, cash flows | [Chartlog](https://www.chartlog.com/product/journal/), [Trademetria](https://trademetria.com/trading-journal) | Reduce manual entry and reconcile accounts | No broker adapters or fills ledger | P3 deferred; requires provider schemas and independent financial validation |

Design synthesis: a compact overview, clear primary action, labeled navigation, grouped filters, a calendar with explicit numbers, and review surfaces connected to source trades. Use an original slate/teal visual system; do not copy branding. Preserve advanced charts in Analytics instead of repeating them on the homepage. Rich text, chart replay and hundreds of widgets add complexity without resolving current workflow gaps.
