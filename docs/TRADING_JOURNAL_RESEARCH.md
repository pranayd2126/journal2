# Research — official sources, 2026-10-21

- [TradeZella](https://www.tradezella.com/trading-journal): calendar, tagging and cross-analysis. Apply a consistent filter population and actionable comparisons.
- [TraderSync](https://tradersync.com/features/): setup comparisons, calendar reporting, risk tracking and granular filters. Prioritize shared filters and visible sample sizes.
- [Edgewonk](https://edgewonk.com/trading-psychology): psychology and discipline review. Compare recorded behavior with outcomes without claiming causality.
- [Tradervue drawdown reports](https://help.tradervue.com/article/4092-drawdown-reports): drawdown periods and recovery are useful beyond net profit. Implement currency drawdown from realized results.
- [TradesViz reference](https://www.tradesviz.com/blog/charts-statistics-reference/): distributions and risk metrics. Use valid recorded stops; keep planned reward/risk separate from realized R.
- [TradesViz AI](https://www.tradesviz.com/ai-trading-journal/): deterministic findings with optional interpretation. Keep financial calculations outside the model.

The application already records useful psychology, execution and mistake fields but does not consistently analyze them. Most valuable additions are trustworthy equity/drawdown, actual R distribution, strategy statistics and overlapping mistake comparisons against an untagged control group. No broker importer, partial-fill ledger, historical equity or verified email delivery exists; do not invent these capabilities.
